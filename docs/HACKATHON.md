## Separate Founder House program

Founder House is October 23–25 in Singapore, with a separate up-to-$300k USDG opportunity. It supports product development, growth and pitching, with potential follow-on mentorship. Applicants are screened; the event targets founders with a prototype/product/business. This is distinct from the online competition and its funding conditions. [Official announcement](https://blog.arbitrum.foundation/founder-house-singapore-apply-now-to-launch-products-on-arbitrum-one-robinhood-chain/)

The combined $415k headline includes both phases; it is not a guaranteed online cash pool. The organizer emphasizes building an MVP, simple infrastructure, and a clear reason for on-chain behavior. ## How Cruxmark should compete

These are our product decisions, not extra organizer requirements:

| Judging concern | Evidence we should offer |
| --- | --- |
| Contract quality | Tested guards, owner-isolated mocks, healthy controls and explicit failure boundaries |
| Real problem | Show exactly how a seeded unit error doubles collateral and unsafe borrowing capacity |
| Innovation | Executable stock-token compatibility regressions tied to a defined integration |
| Market fit | Clear protocol-engineer buyer; honest interview/pricing status |
| Chain use | Judges can inspect real testnet deployment and fault/protection transactions |

Robinhood Chain Testnet is our primary target because stock-token semantics are the product's focus. Sepolia is the fallback. We do not need Stylus, a custom chain or USDG to implement the core scenario. Add genuine USDG only if its network/address availability and useful role are verified; an invented/mock-branded USDG integration would weaken the submission.
